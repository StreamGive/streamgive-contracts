# NNNN. Short title that states the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Applies to:** `donation-vault`, `ngo-registry`, or both

## Context

What problem or force led to this decision? Describe the situation and the
constraints (cost, security, compatibility) in a few sentences. State facts,
not the conclusion.

## Decision

What was decided? Write it in the active voice: "The vault does X." Name the
functions, types, or constants involved so a reader can find them.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision, good and bad?

- What it makes easier or safer.
- What it makes harder, costlier, or riskier.
- What a future change must keep in mind.

## Alternatives considered

Other options that were weighed, and why they were not chosen. Omit this
section if there were none worth recording.

## References

Links to code, issues, pull requests, and related docs.
